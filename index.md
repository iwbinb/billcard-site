# BillCard

Track credit card bills locally. No account. No server. No ads. No tracking.

Your card data never leaves your iPhone.

---

## What BillCard does

- Track multiple credit cards with statement day, due day, credit limit, and annual fee
- Record bills and partial payments; see remaining balance at a glance
- Face ID lock so anyone who grabs your phone can't browse your cards
- Credit utilization ring that turns green / orange / red as you spend
- Local notifications on statement and due days, with "Mark Paid" and "Remind Tomorrow" right from the lock screen
- Auto-generated annual fee bills
- English and Simplified Chinese with on-the-fly switching
- Six months of billed vs. paid charts, plus outstanding-by-card breakdown
- JSON backup and restore

## What BillCard doesn't do

- Connect to your bank
- Collect any data
- Show ads
